We are excited to welcome Dr. Shashi Singh to the Witten Lab as a postdoctoral researcher. Shashi's research will develop scalable platforms for targeted RNA delivery and gene editing by combining nanoparticle engineering with high-throughput screening. He earned his PhD from Jawaharlal Nehru University and was previously a postdoctoral fellow with Dr. Joseph Luna at Case Western Reserve University and Cleveland Clinic.
