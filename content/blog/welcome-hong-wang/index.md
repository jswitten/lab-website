We are excited to welcome Hong Wang to the Witten Lab. Hong is a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at Case Western Reserve University, joining us from another lab on campus. His research will develop high-throughput screening platforms for targeted systemic drug delivery, combining nanoparticle engineering with multiplexed ex vivo and in vivo biological assays. He brings expertise in long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) and immune responses in cancer from his collaborative research with Dr. William P. Schiemann.
