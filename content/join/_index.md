        **The Witten Lab is not currently hiring, with the exception of CWRU undergraduate researchers.**

        Our work spans:

        - Artificial intelligence
        - Nanoparticle formulation and characterization
        - Gene therapy or nucleic acid delivery
        - High-throughput experimentation
        - Molecular biology
        - Synthetic chemistry
        - Airway biology
        - Regenerative medicine
        - Virology
