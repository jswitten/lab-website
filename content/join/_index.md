        **The Witten Lab is not currently hiring, with the exception of CWRU undergraduate researchers and potentially CWRU master's students.**

        We are not recruiting postdocs or staff at this time, and we are not taking on new PhD students outside of the normal program rotations. This page will be updated when positions open up.

        Our work spans:

        - Artificial intelligence
        - Nanoparticle formulation and characterization
        - Gene therapy or nucleic acid delivery
        - High-throughput experimentation
        - Molecular biology
        - Synthetic chemistry
        - Airway biology
        - Regenerative medicine
        - Virology

        We are potentially accepting CWRU master's students. If you are a CWRU master's student interested in research opportunities, please email Dr. Witten with your resume and a brief description of your interests.
